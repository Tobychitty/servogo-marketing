Routing

The shortest path through the day.

Servogo plots every stop on a live map and orders them so your van isn’t criss-crossing the postcode. Re-optimize when the day shifts.

  • One-tap recalculate. Job runs long, customer cancels, weather turns — press the button, the day re-sorts.
  • Live progress, no chatter. See what’s done, what’s next, and how the day is tracking against the window.
  • Hand off to navigation. Tap a stop, jump straight to Google Maps or Apple Maps. No copy-paste addresses.

Invoicing

Paid faster, with less faff.

A finished job is half an invoice. Servogo writes the other half — line items, totals, due date — the moment the visit closes.

  • Outstanding, in one place. Overdue and due-soon side by side, sorted by how long they’ve been waiting.
  • Reminders that send themselves. Polite nudge at +3 days, firm one at +7. You can override the tone or pause for a client.
  • Pay any way. Card link in the email, BACS for businesses, mark cash on the doorstep. No reconciliation by hand.

Questions

Asked & answered.

If something else is on your mind, the team replies in hours, not days. No bot triage.

Can I import my clients from Jobber or Housecall Pro?
Yes. Drop a CSV export and we map clients, addresses, services, and recurring schedules in a single pass. The trial includes a free white-glove import — we’ll do it for you on a screen-share.
Does it work without signal?
Yes. The crew app caches the day’s route, jobs, and notes locally. Update a job offline and it syncs the second you’re back in coverage — nothing is lost in a basement or a cellar.
Do my technicians need their own paid accounts?
No. On the Crew plan and up, technicians are unlimited and free. We charge for the business, not the headcount, so adding a new hire is a button click, never an upsell.
Can I export my data if I leave?
Always. Every list view exports to CSV. The full account — clients, jobs, invoices, attachments — downloads as a single zip from Settings. No support tickets, no negotiation.
How long is the trial?
14 days, no card up front. You get the full Crew plan during the trial — route optimization, unlimited techs, the lot. Convert when you’re ready, not when a timer says so.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. One click in Settings. No retention call, no “are you sure” gauntlet. We pro-rate the unused portion of the month back to your card.
