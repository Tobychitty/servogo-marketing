Organization Preferences contain the defaults that apply across the app.

- **Currency** - used everywhere monetary values appear.
- **Country and locale** - date format, address format, distance units.
- **Timezone** - controls when "today" rolls over and what time scheduled jobs land at.
- **Working days** - defaults for new personnel and for the route planner.
- **Day start time** - default start of the working day on Day View.
- **Default service duration** - used when a service has no explicit duration set.

> Changing currency or timezone affects display, not historical records. Past invoices keep the currency they were issued in.
