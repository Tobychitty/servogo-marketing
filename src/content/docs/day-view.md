Day View is where the day's work actually happens. It is the default landing page for dispatchers and techs, and the page most people spend most of their time on.

## The layout

- **Date and tech** selector at the top. Pick the day and (for admins) the technician whose route you're viewing.
- **Route timeline** on the left - every stop with arrival and departure times.
- **Map** showing the route line and stop pins.
- **Weather panel** with hour-by-hour forecast for the route's location.
- **Stats row** across the top: stops, total distance, drive time, on-site time, day length.

## Reordering stops

Drag any stop up or down in the timeline. Times update as you drag - you can see the impact on arrival times before you let go. The map redraws automatically.

## Optimising the route

Click **Optimise** in the toolbar. Servogo finds the shortest tour through all stops, starting and ending at your home address (if "include home" is on). Optimisation is non-destructive - you can always drag back to a manual order.

## Marking jobs done

Click a stop, then **Mark complete**. The visit is recorded immediately and feeds into invoicing. Techs do the same thing from their mobile view.

## Breaks and start time

Set the day's start time using the time picker above the timeline. Add a break by clicking between two stops - it inserts a gap that pushes later stops down accordingly.

## Weather

The weather panel shows the forecast at the centroid of your route. If you're rain-sensitive (window cleaners, painters), use it to decide whether to push the day or proceed.

> **Tip:** Servogo persists your manual stop order. Reopening Day View later in the day shows the same order you set in the morning, even if you've re-optimised since.
