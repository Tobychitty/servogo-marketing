---

Branding controls how your business appears to clients - on invoices, emails, and customer-facing pages.

- **Business name** - what clients see on invoices and emails.
- **Logo** - uploaded as PNG or SVG. Appears on invoices and emails.
- **Primary colour** - used as the accent on customer-facing pages.
- **Address and contact** - shown on invoice footers.
- **Email "from" name** - the sender name on outgoing client emails.

> **Tip:** Use a square logo at least 256x256 pixels. PNGs with transparent backgrounds look cleanest on email and PDF headers.
