Billing is your subscription to Servogo itself - separate from your own invoicing of your clients. Only owners can see this section.

## What's here

- Current plan and price.
- Next billing date.
- Payment method.
- Past invoices (yours, paid to Servogo).
- Buttons to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel.

## What happens if a payment fails

Your account moves to **past due**. You stay in read-only mode for 7 days while we retry the card. After that, the account is paused. See [Billing states](/docs/concepts-billing-states) for the full lifecycle.

## Cancellation

Cancel from this page. Your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently deleted. Export anything you need before then.
