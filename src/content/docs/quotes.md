Quotes let you give a price for work before you commit to scheduling it. When the client accepts, you can convert the quote to a job in one click.

## Drafting a quote

1. Quotes → **New quote**.
2. Pick or create the client.
3. Add line items: description, quantity, unit price.
4. Add notes (validity period, terms, scope).
5. Save as draft, or **Send** to email it directly to the client.

## Tracking status

A quote is in one of these states: **Draft**, **Sent**, **Accepted**, **Declined**, or **Expired**. Clients can accept online via the link in the email; you can also mark accepted manually after a phone call.

## Converting to a job

Open an accepted quote and click **Convert to job**. The line items become the service, and you pick a date and tech. The quote stays linked to the resulting job for reference.
