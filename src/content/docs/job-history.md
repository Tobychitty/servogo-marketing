Job History is a searchable record of every visit your business has completed. Useful for answering "when were we last there?" questions and for compiling a client's service record.

## Filters

- **Date range** - last 30 days, last quarter, custom.
- **Client** - visits to a specific client.
- **Tech** - visits performed by a specific person.
- **Service** - only window cleans, only gutter clears, etc.

## Exporting

Hit **Export** to download the filtered list as CSV. Handy for compiling year-end reports or moving data into accounting software.
