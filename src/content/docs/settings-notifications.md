Notifications controls automated messages: when they go out, what they say, and which channels are enabled.

## Automated messages

- **Visit reminders** - sent N hours before a scheduled visit.
- **Route updates** - sent if the tech is running early or late.
- **Invoice sent** - confirmation when an invoice is emailed.
- **Payment received** - receipt when an invoice is paid.

## Editing templates

Click any template to edit. You can use placeholders - for example `{client_name}` or `{visit_date}` - and Servogo substitutes them at send time.

## Turning notifications on or off

Each notification type can be switched on or off independently. Notifications are sent by email, so the client needs a valid email address on their profile to receive them.
