Schedule is where you set up *what* work is happening, separate from *when* it happens day-to-day. Most ongoing client work lives here as a service plan; one-off work is created here too.

## The four tabs

- **Recurring** - service plans on a repeating cadence (every 4 weeks, monthly, etc.).
- **One-off** - single jobs that aren't part of a plan.
- **Requests** - incoming work requests waiting to be scheduled.
- **Ended** - plans you've stopped (cancelled, lost, paused).

## Creating a recurring service plan

1. Schedule → Recurring → **New plan**.
2. Pick the client.
3. Choose the service from your catalog (or add a new one).
4. Set the frequency: every N weeks/months, or specific days.
5. Set the price and the tech to assign.
6. Set the start date. Future jobs are generated automatically.

## Creating a one-off job

One-off → **New job**. Pick a client, service, date, and tech. It appears on the route on its scheduled day.

## Editing or cancelling a plan

Click any plan to open the editor. You can change frequency, price, or assigned tech. Use **End plan** to stop generating future jobs - the plan moves to the Ended tab and historical visits stay intact.

## Requests

If a client submits a request (or you log one manually), it lands in the Requests tab. Click to convert it into a plan or a one-off job.
