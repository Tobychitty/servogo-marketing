Your Servogo subscription moves through a small number of states. Knowing them helps explain why some buttons might be disabled.

## Active

The normal state. Everything works.

## Past due

We tried to charge your card and it failed. Your account is read-only - you can still view everything, mark jobs complete, and download invoices, but you can't send new invoices or edit settings. We retry the card daily for 7 days. Update the payment method in Settings → Billing to recover immediately.

## Paused

After 7 days past-due, the account is paused. Same restrictions as past-due, plus we stop generating new automated emails. Update the card to resume.

## Canceled

You cancelled, or we cancelled after extended non-payment. The account is read-only for 30 days, then deleted. Export anything you need within that window.
