Servogo has four roles. Each invited team member is assigned one. You can change a person's role at any time from Settings → Personnel.

## Roles

- **Owner** - full access. Sees everything, can invite anyone, manages billing and the subscription. There is always at least one owner.
- **Manager** - full access except billing. Can edit schedule, clients, invoices, personnel.
- **Dispatcher** - manages schedule, routes, clients, and visits. Cannot manage personnel or invoices.
- **Tech** - sees only their own routes and jobs. Marks visits complete from the field. Cannot see other techs' work, clients they aren't visiting, or any financials.

## Inviting someone

1. Settings → Personnel → **Invite**.
2. Enter their email and pick a role.
3. They receive an email with a sign-in link valid for 7 days.
4. When they accept, they appear under Personnel as Active.

> If an invitation expires, click **Resend** from the Personnel list.

## Removing someone

Settings → Personnel → click the person → **Remove**. Their historical work (assigned visits, invoices created, etc.) stays intact - you just lose the ability to assign them new work.
