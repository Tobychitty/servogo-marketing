Servogo keeps access simple. Every team member is either an **admin** or a **standard** team member. You can change someone's access level at any time from Settings → Personnel. The person who signs up is the account **owner** - an admin who can't be removed.

## The two access levels

- **Admin** - full access. Sees the whole business: every team member's schedule and routes, clients, quotes, invoices, job history, payroll, personnel, settings, and billing.
- **Standard** - field-focused. Sees their own day on Day View, plus their own clients and job history, and marks their own visits complete. They don't see the dashboard, the full schedule, quotes, invoices, payroll, personnel, or settings.

## Inviting someone

1. Settings → Personnel → **Add team member**.
2. Enter their name and email, and choose whether they're an admin.
3. They receive an email with a sign-in link valid for 7 days.
4. When they set their password, they appear under Personnel as Active.

> If an invitation expires, click **Resend invite** from the Personnel list to send a fresh 7-day link.

## Changing access or removing someone

Open a team member from Personnel to switch them between admin and standard, or to remove them. Their historical work (assigned visits, invoices created, earnings) stays intact - you just stop assigning them new work.
