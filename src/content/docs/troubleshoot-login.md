## Can't sign in

- Check the email address - typos are the most common cause.
- Use **Forgot password** to set a new one.
- If you've just been invited, check that the invitation hasn't expired (7 days).
- Check spam folders for the password reset or invitation email.

## Reset link doesn't work

Reset links expire after 60 minutes. Request another. If that one also fails, it usually means the link was opened in a different browser than the one you're trying to sign in from - open the email in the same browser you're using.

## Session keeps logging me out

Servogo uses 30-minute access tokens with automatic refresh. If you keep getting kicked out, your browser may be blocking local storage. Whitelist `servogo.co.uk`.

## Still stuck

Email support@servogo.co.uk with the address you're trying to sign in with and a screenshot of the error.
