The Dashboard is where owners and managers get a snapshot of the business. It is the default landing page for admin roles.

## What you see

- **KPI cards** - outstanding invoices, this week's revenue, completed visits, upcoming jobs.
- **Cashflow chart** - invoiced vs. paid over your selected window.
- **Recent activity** - the latest completed visits and sent invoices.

## Date range

Use the date range selector at the top to switch between week, month, quarter, and custom windows. The KPIs and chart update together.

> **Tip:** Friday afternoon is a good time to glance at the Dashboard. Outstanding invoices more than 30 days old usually mean it's time for a polite chase email.
