You create your account and your organization at the same time. The person who signs up becomes the **owner** - full access to everything including billing.

## Sign up

1. Open the app and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your name, email, and a password.
3. Enter your company name. This becomes your organization.
4. Confirm your email by clicking the link we send you.

> Already have an account but want to start a second business? Email support@servogo.co.uk - one user can be a member of more than one organization.

## What happens next

You'll land on the Dashboard with an empty state. The next step is the first-day checklist - five steps that get you to a working schedule.
